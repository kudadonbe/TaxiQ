# How to play TaxiQ Game

TaxiQ Game is a taxi business game on the real island map. You play with play money, and the real
taxi service is never touched. It's being built in phases, and this guide says what you can do today
and what's coming. (Game rules for the builders are in [game-mode.md](game-mode.md).)

## The goal: get "off the meter"

Everyone starts out living from their own work: a salary, or driving fares. You win when your
**passive income** (money that comes in without you working, such as cars you rent to other drivers or
your center's earnings) is **more than your monthly expenses**. Driving pays the bills; owning things
sets you free.

## What you can do now (phase G1)

### 1. Open the game
Go to **https://kd-taxiq.web.app**, sign in with Google, and open the **🎮 Game** tab.

### 2. Start or join a game
- **Create a game:** give it a name and pick a size. **Standard** suits up to 8 players, with 20 cars, 2 centers and KD-Bank holding MVR 6 million. Tick **Fill empty roles with bots** to play with fewer people.
- You get a **6-letter join code**. Press **Share** to send it to friends.
- **Join a game:** type the code a friend sent you and press **Join**.
- **My games** lists the games you've joined on this device, so you can go back to them.

### 3. Pick your role
In the room, choose **Your role**:

| Role | You are | You'll want to |
|------|---------|----------------|
| **Citizen** | Someone with a job and a salary who needs rides | Save money and buy or lease your first car |
| **Driver** | A taxi driver in a center's queue | Earn fares, keep costs down, then buy more cars |
| **Center owner** | The owner of a taxi center | Grow your fleet and drivers, and earn from every trip |

You can change your role until the host starts the game. Bots take any role nobody chose.

### 4. Start the game (host)
The person who created the game is the **host**. The host presses **Start game**, and can later **Pause**, **Resume** or **End** it.

### 5. Watch the island
- The map shows the game's cars driving on the real roads. **Everyone sees the same cars in the same places.**
- The **game clock** runs 1 real minute = 1 game hour, starting at 06:00 on Day 1. Pausing stops it.
- Use the map like the real app: pinch or scroll to zoom, two fingers (or right-drag) to rotate, and tap a plot to see its houses.

## Coming next

| Phase | What you'll be able to do |
|-------|---------------------------|
| **G2: Rides** | Citizens (you or bots) **hire a game taxi** by picking a pickup and destination. Offers go down the center's queue exactly like real TaxiQ. Drivers **accept or decline**, and the car drives the route to the pickup and destination. The trip completes and the fare is paid at **real fares**. Drivers can **send their empty car to a waiting spot** (the harbour, the hospital). |
| **G3: Money and KD-Bank** | Your **statement**: income, expenses, assets, loans. **Payday** every game month. Money in **Dinar (gold) and Dirham (silver)** at live prices, or digital MVR. **KD-Bank**, Islamic finance with no interest: buy a car with **Murabaha**, lease to own with **Ijara**, co-own a center with **Musharaka**, or take an interest-free **Qard Hasan** in hard times. |
| **G4: Winning** | The **off-the-meter bar**, winning and bankruptcy, a scoreboard, and saving and resuming games. |
| **G5: Life on the island** | Events (festival nights, rain, fuel prices, a flat tyre), deals between players (sell or rent a car, hire an operator), and choosing a route for each trip. |

## Fair play

- It's play money only. Nothing can be bought, sold or cashed out for real.
- KD-Bank treats every player by the same rules, and charges no interest.
- Every payment goes into a permanent ledger that everyone in the room can check (from G3).
- In this first version the host's phone runs the game, so play with people you trust. The later server version makes it fully fair.
