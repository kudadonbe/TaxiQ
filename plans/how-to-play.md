# How to play TaxiQ Game

*Dedicated to Mohamed bin Hussain.*

TaxiQ Game is a taxi business game on the real island map. You play with play money, and the real
taxi service is never touched. It's being built in phases, and this guide says what you can do today
and what's coming. (Game rules for the builders are in [game-mode.md](game-mode.md).)

## The goal: learn the job, earn, and know you can do it for real

Play as a driver: go online, take ride offers, drive the real roads and charge **real fares**. You pay
**real costs** too (fuel, your car, the center fee). Every trip and every cost goes into your
**driver's logbook**, and your **earnings report** shows what you took home each day, week and month.

When you finish, you'll know in real numbers what driving a taxi on this island earns, and what makes
it earn more: waiting in the right places at the right times, keeping costs down, and good ratings.
The figures are estimates from the game, not a promise, but they use real prices.

**Want more?** The long-term goal is getting **"off the meter"**: owning cars or a center so your
**passive income** is more than your monthly expenses.

## What you can do now (phases G1 and G2)

### 1. Open the game
Go to **https://kd-taxiq.web.app**, sign in with Google, and open the **🎮 Game** tab.

### 2. Enter TaxiQ World, or a private world
- **TaxiQ World** is always there and always running: Kulhudhuffushi, with its own drivers, taxi centers and citizens who never go away. Press **Enter the world** to play, alone or with everyone else who's in.
- **Create a game:** give it a name and pick a size. **Standard** suits up to 8 players, with 20 cars, 2 centers and KD-Bank holding MVR 6 million. Tick **Fill empty roles with bots** to play with fewer people.
- You get a **6-letter join code**. Press **Share** to send it to friends.
- **Join a game:** type the code a friend sent you and press **Join**.
- **My games** lists the games you've joined on this device, so you can go back to them.

### 3. Pick your role
In the room, choose **Your role**:

| Role | You are | You'll want to |
|------|---------|----------------|
| **Citizen** | Someone with a job and a salary who needs rides | Save money and buy or lease your first car |
| **Driver** | A taxi driver in a center's queue | Earn fares, keep costs down, then buy more cars |
| **Operator** | A center's dispatcher at the radio | Watch the queue and keep rides moving |
| **Center owner** | The owner of a taxi center | Grow your fleet and drivers, and earn from every trip |

You can change your role at any time. Developers get a **View as** bar to switch between all four roles in one tap. Bots take any role nobody chose.

### 4. Start the game (host)
The person who created the game is the **host**. The host presses **Start game**, and can later **Pause**, **Resume** or **End** it.

### 5. Play your role
- **Driver:** press **Online**. Ride offers come in **queue order**, like at a real center: the car that has waited longest gets the next ride. You have **20 seconds** to **Accept** or **Decline**. Your car then drives itself to the pickup and on to the destination, and the fare goes into your **logbook**. You go to the back of the queue. When you're free, choose **Wait at** to drive to a spot such as the harbour.
- **Citizen:** choose a **pickup** and **destination** from the house search and press **Book the ride**. You see which car is coming, when it arrives, and the fare.
- **Operator:** the center's dispatcher. The **queue board** shows the rides waiting, which car is being asked, the queue in order (the top car gets the next ride), the cars on a job and the cars offline.
- **Center owner:** watch each center's trips and fares.
- **Logbook:** every finished trip with its time, places, km and fare, plus your totals.

### 6. Watch the island
- The map shows the game's cars driving on the real roads, and your car is highlighted. **Everyone sees the same cars in the same places.**
- The **game clock** runs 1 real minute = 6 game minutes, starting at 06:00 on Day 1.
- Fares are **placeholders** until the real Kulhudhuffushi fare table is in: MVR 20 + 5 per km, at least 25, and 10 more at night.
- Use the map like the real app: pinch or scroll to zoom, two fingers (or right-drag) to rotate, and tap a plot to see its houses.

## Coming next

|-------|---------------------------|
| ✅ **G2: Rides and your logbook (done)** | Every trip and fare is written into your **driver's logbook**. Citizens (you or bots) **hire a game taxi** by picking a pickup and destination. ## Fair play

- It's play money only. Nothing can be bought, sold or cashed out for real.
- KD-Bank treats every player by the same rules, and charges no interest.
- Every payment goes into a permanent ledger that everyone in the room can check (from G3).
- In v2 one player's phone runs the world at a time (it passes on when they leave), so a changed app could cheat. The later server version makes it fully fair.
